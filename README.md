# Customer Purchase Behavior Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior to understand purchasing patterns, product category performance, discount usage, subscription status, seasonal trends, and preferred payment methods.

The objective is to extract meaningful business insights from customer purchase data and provide data-driven recommendations to support business decision-making.

## 2. Business Problem

Businesses need to understand how customers purchase products, which categories perform better, and how discounts, subscriptions, seasons, and payment preferences relate to purchasing behavior.

This project explores the following business questions:

- Which product categories have the highest average purchase amount?
- How does purchasing behavior vary by gender and age group?
- How does average purchase amount differ between discounted and non-discounted purchases?
- Do subscribers and non-subscribers show different purchasing patterns?
- Which seasons have higher average purchase amounts?
- Which payment methods are associated with higher average purchase amounts?
- Which products are most frequently purchased?

## 3. Tools and Technologies

- **MySQL:** Data querying, aggregation, and business analysis.

## 4. Dataset Description

The project uses a customer shopping trends dataset containing 3,900 records.

The dataset includes information about customer demographics, purchased items, product categories, purchase amounts, discounts, subscriptions, seasons, and preferred payment methods.

The dataset is used for analytical practice and portfolio development. It should not be interpreted as verified data from a real company's transactions.

## 6. Key Business Insights

The initial SQL analysis produced the following findings:

- **Seasonal trends:** Fall had an average purchase amount of 61.56, the highest among the seasons in the reported results.
- **Gender comparison:** The reported averages were 60.25 for female customers and 59.54 for male customers.
- **Category comparison:** Footwear had a reported average purchase amount of 60.26, while Outerwear had 57.17.
- **Discount comparison:** Discounted purchases averaged 59.28, compared with 60.13 for non-discounted purchases.
- **Subscription comparison:** Subscribers averaged 59.49, while non-subscribers averaged 59.87.
- **Payment methods:** Venmo had a reported average purchase amount of 61.24.

These are descriptive findings from the analyzed sample. Differences do not establish that any customer characteristic or business practice caused a change in purchase amount.

## 7. Business Recommendations

### Seasonal Marketing
Test targeted seasonal campaigns during high-performing periods and measure their impact against a suitable comparison group.

### Product Category Performance
Investigate product mix, pricing, demand, and customer preferences to understand differences between categories.

### Discount Strategy
Evaluate targeted discount experiments using purchase amount, conversion, and profit margin rather than average purchase amount alone.

### Subscription Program
Assess subscription performance using repeat purchase frequency, retention, and customer lifetime value.

### Payment Methods
Compare payment methods using customer adoption, transaction success, and processing costs before changing payment options or promotions.

## 9. Limitations

- The dataset is sample data and may not represent actual business behavior.
- The analysis is descriptive and does not establish causal relationships.
- The available fields may not include profit, acquisition cost, or customer lifetime value.
- Business recommendations should be tested and validated before implementation.
- Reported figures should be reconciled with the final SQL queries and Power BI measures before publication.
